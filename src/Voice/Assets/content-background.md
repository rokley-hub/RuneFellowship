# Main content background

Original AI-generated charcoal material created for Rune Fellowship on 10 September 2026, matching the user-approved Play page preview. Fine matte grain and faint bronze ambient shading replace the busy leather pattern only in the main content column. No third-party game artwork was used for this asset.

The shared background sits behind all main pages, fills the right column as the window resizes, and does not intercept input. The original sidebar illustration, shield, logo, top bar and footer remain separate and unchanged.
