# Rune shelters

Three original, ground-level PlanBuild designs. Each contains exactly one workbench and one bed inside, one campfire outside, walls and a thatched roof. Furniture sits against opposite side walls with a clear central aisle and an open entrance. There are no extra forges, floors, terrain edits, scaled pieces or free resources.

| Stage | Name | Command | Extra prerequisite |
|---|---|---|---|
| Early | Camp Shelter | `planbuild camp shelter on me` | Discovered wood and stone |
| Mid | Stone Shelter | `planbuild stone shelter on me` | Known stone construction; a stonecutter already in range |
| Late | Marble Shelter | `planbuild marble shelter on me` | Discovered black marble; a stonecutter already in range |

Replace `on me` with `on yourself` to use Rune's position and facing. Stand at the centre of the intended entrance and face into the building. Allow approximately 11 × 10 metres of clear, level, dry ground for Camp and Marble, or 11 × 12 metres for Stone, including the outdoor fire and working space. Stone uses full-height stone side walls with timber ends. These blueprints do not level terrain. The entrance is open so neither the player nor the companion is trapped behind a closed door.

The live crafting checklist is authoritative for your installed mods' resource costs. The native recipe baseline is 72 wood and 5 stone for Camp (29 pieces); 66 wood and 29 stone for Stone (30 pieces); and 50 wood, 5 stone and 88 black marble for Marble (40 pieces). Rune also needs her own usable hammer; that tool is not included in blueprint costs. Difficult resources must be available through supported gathering or accessible storage. Rune does not automate every production chain, smelting process or unloaded journey.

For supplies in your own chests, first say `set base here` near them and enable stored-material use. Rune physically fetches bounded batches and deposits them into the real PlanBuild pieces. She can make multiple trips. Missing tools, support, station range or a safe route produce a blocked explanation rather than a completion claim.

For a blueprint you have already placed, stand within 25 metres and say `build this blueprint` or `finish plan`. This selects your nearby unfinished PlanBuild pieces, potentially including more than one design. Named requests create a new copy; `finish plan` resumes an existing one. The existing limit is 256 pieces per request. Stay near the site so it remains loaded.

`follow me` aborts work and follows you. `clear crafting` clears the job. Unfinished plans and deposited resources remain in the world; unspent supplies stay in Rune's inventory or storage.

Beds obey native shelter, fire, ownership and sleep conditions. The outdoor fire still needs fuel and can be affected by weather. A standalone `build a workbench` creates only a bench; that bench still needs shelter before it can craft or repair.

Rune installs these files into the selected Rune-owned mod profile. Older designs and personal backups are not part of this release. The source package includes tools to regenerate and statically check these designs.
